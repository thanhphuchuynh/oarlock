# Landing page

Scope: the marketing surface at `landing/`. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

Audience: developers self-hosting first, operations/risk second. Job: decide whether the
security model survives their scepticism, then run the quickstart. Primary action: the
quickstart. Secondary: talk to us.

Proof on hand: the README transcript, the three diagrams in `docs/diagrams/`, threat-model
§ 4 and § 12. No customers, logos, benchmarks or adoption numbers exist — none may be
invented.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The product drawn as a utility invention under examination: every component
numbered, every numeral resolving to the document that specifies it. Refuses the
near-black hero with a glowing terminal that this category always ships.

**OWN-WORLD.** India-ink hairlines on warm bond. Three inks, no tint ramp: ink, hatch
grey, carmine. Carmine is reserved for what is under examination — here, what is **not
built**. Ruled sheet border, metadata rail down the left, title block top-right,
registration marks as section markers, section hatching as the only fill, one dated seal.
Spaced-caps serif display; letterspaced small-caps labels; tabular figures.

**STORY.** An engineer understands that the gateway terminates SSH itself and the device
only dials out; believes it because every claim carries a numeral into a real document,
and because the page states the cost before the benefit; leaves by running the quickstart.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Ruled sheet edge-to-edge. Left rail: licence, protocol version, status,
class. Headline in spaced serif caps, two lines, upper left. Beneath it the cost sentence,
then two ruled buttons. Right two-thirds: Fig. 1, the gateway exploded into numbered parts
on curved leaders. Far right: the reference-numeral table, its carmine row the one thing
not built. Dated seal bottom-left.

**FORM.** Utility patent drawing sheet; the challenger that beat assigned index 5 on both
axes; seed 6f60dd8a.

**FINISH.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish
review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
